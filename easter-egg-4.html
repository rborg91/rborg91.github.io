Emerging Leaders Program - Learnings and Key Takeaways

  • The Self-Aware Leader
    • Know your team
    • Know when to direct and when to support
  • The Teaming Leader
    • Constructive feedback - win/win situation
    • Clear is kind
  • The Coaching Leader
    • Active listening
    • Listening to understand
    • Ask questions to understand
  • The Courageous Leader
    • Having a good conversation - allow space for silence
    • Develop your network
